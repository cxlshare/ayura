OUR STORY

Born from Responsibility

Ayura began with a mission shaped by a deeply personal experience. As engineers, our founders saw firsthand how modern food supply chains can rely on mass processing, chemical alteration and adulteration.

That experience led us to look more closely at what we consume and to build a brand around a simple responsibility: bring authentic, unadulterated oils back to the modern kitchen.

We combine an engineer's precision for quality with a deep respect for traditional wellness — because what reaches your pantry should be something you can trust.

“

We aren't just building a business; we are reclaiming the purity of what we put into our bodies.

OUR MISSION

Restore Integrity to the Modern Kitchen

Our mission is to combat food adulteration by delivering 100% pure, unrefined, cold-pressed oils crafted with meticulous care and an unwavering responsibility to human health.

We believe access to clean, unadulterated food is a fundamental right, not a luxury. Through authentic groundnut, sesame and sunflower oils, we aim to bring transparency and integrity back to everyday cooking.

01

Solvent-Free Process

No chemical solvents, artificial preservatives or blending agents. Every drop is first-press oil extracted through gentle mechanical means.

02

Meticulous Craftsmanship

Rigorous cleanliness standards, temperature controls and quality checks guide our traditional extraction methods.

03

Transparent Process

We aim for complete honesty in how our oils are pressed, handled and packed — with nothing hidden and nothing added.

OUR PACKAGING

Purpose-Built Tin Bottles

Our commitment to protecting the oil continues beyond the press. We deliberately avoid plastic and clear glass and package Ayura oils in premium tin bottles.

01

Light & UV Protection

The bottles act as a blackout shield, helping protect the oil's nutrients, natural antioxidants and fresh aroma from light exposure.

02

Stable & Inert

Our packaging is designed to avoid the chemical-leaching concerns associated with plastic, especially in warm kitchen environments.

03

Durable & Recyclable

The tin bottles are durable and recyclable, aligning our focus on human health with responsibility toward the planet.
