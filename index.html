OUR STORY

Born from
Responsibility

Before Ayura was a brand, it was a mission born from a stark reality. Our founders' personal experience with chronic illness changed their perspective on what we consume.

We saw how modern food supply chains can rely on mass-processing, chemical alteration and adulteration. We founded Ayura to fight food adulteration at its root and provide families with an ingredient they can trust.

We believe the healthiest path forward often requires a step back: returning to traditional cold pressing and preserving the natural character of the oil without the harsh processing associated with refined oils.
