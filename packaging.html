OUR PACKAGING

Purpose-Built Tin Bottles

Protecting the oil is part of protecting what reaches your pantry.

We deliberately avoid plastic and clear glass, packaging all Ayura oils in premium tin bottles.

01

Light & UV Protection

Our bottles act as a blackout shield, helping protect the oil from light-driven oxidation and preserve its natural character.

02

Stable & Inert

Purpose-built tin packaging is designed to avoid the concerns associated with plastic leaching.

03

Durable & Recyclable

Our bottles are durable and recyclable, aligning our passion for human health with responsibility to the planet.
