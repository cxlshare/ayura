OUR STORY

Born from Responsibility

Before Ayura was a brand, it was a mission born from a stark reality.

As engineers, our founders' experiences across countries and within their own family changed their perspective on what we consume.

We saw how modern food supply chains can rely on mass-processed, chemically altered and adulterated ingredients. We knew we could not simply sit by.

Combining an engineer's precision for quality with a deeply personal commitment to wellness, we founded Ayura to fight food adulteration at its root and provide families with an ingredient they can trust.

“We aren't just building a business; we are reclaiming the purity of what we put into our bodies.”

RETURNING TO THE ROOTS OF PURITY

A step back to what oil should be.

At Ayura, we believe the healthiest path forward often requires a step back. We set out to bring authentic, cold-pressed oils back to the modern kitchen.

Our process is simple but meticulous: carefully selected oilseeds, traditional mechanical pressing and zero refinement.
